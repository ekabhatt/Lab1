A Brief History of Halloween

Halloween traces back over two thousand years to the ancient Celtic festival of Samhain, when people believed the boundary between the living and the dead grew thin. Bonfires were lit and costumes were worn to ward off wandering spirits. When Christianity spread through Celtic lands, the holiday blended with All Hallows' Eve, the night before All Saints' Day, and slowly took on the name we use today. Irish and Scottish immigrants carried these traditions to America in the 1800s, where trick-or-treating, costumes, and carved pumpkins eventually grew into the holiday celebrated every October 31st.
