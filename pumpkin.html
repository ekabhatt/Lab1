The Curse of the Jack-O-Lantern

Long ago, an old tale was told of a trickster named Jack who outwitted the Devil himself, and was cursed to wander the earth for it, forever carrying only a single burning coal inside a hollowed turnip to light his way through the dark. When Irish immigrants arrived in America, they found pumpkins far easier to carve than turnips, and the jack-o-lantern was born.
